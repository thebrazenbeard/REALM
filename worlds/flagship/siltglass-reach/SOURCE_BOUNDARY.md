# Siltglass Reach — source and rights boundary

**Evidence class: authored original showcase, informed by private research.**

The concept was developed after read-only review of private tabletop-campaign
planning notes made accessible by the project owner. Only broad design patterns
were used as prompts: a port economy, two competing social hubs, disputed civic
authority, unreliable paths, environmental uncertainty, and evidence-driven
player decisions. These are generic storytelling techniques, not imported
setting data or claims of authorship over the underlying reference material.

The private notes intermingle original campaign invention with identifiable
third-party settings, names, monsters, deities, rules, items, and familiar
fantasy properties. Possession of a local file is **not** proof of permission
to republish its content. Accordingly:

- The source files and their contents are **not** committed to this repository.
- No named campaign faction, party member, player character, NPC, tavern,
  deity, location, map, plot scene, dialogue, riddle text, or rule stat block
  was copied into Siltglass Reach.
- All showcased geography, room prose, civic groups, portable items, and
  central dispute were drafted anew for REALM.
- No source world or literary canon is imported, inferred, or declared
  authoritative by this pack.
- The packed JSON is passive content. No model, script, hidden quest,
  public network endpoint, or host authorization is embedded.

The optional `living_lore.v1.json` extension is likewise original writing
and deliberately outside the engine's import schema. Its fictional residents,
factions, barter tallies, atmospheric states and quest questions are prompts
for player-authored activity, not imported characters, working AI agents,
autonomous mechanics, real currencies, or rights claims over historical MUDs.
No private Morax Marauders, All Good Things, Driftspace, White Wolf, Melmoth,
Astaria, GodWars or Vampire Wars names, monster statistics, maps, item text,
dialogue or rule systems were transcribed into it.

This is a creation/provenance note, **not** a legal rights opinion or an
independent originality certification. Before reusing any private campaign
material more closely, obtain explicit rights and a separate editorial review.
